covtl-sandhi v0.1.0 — inter-word sandhi demos

Self-contained articulatory simulations (COVTL + VocalTractLab, speaker jd3, WAV embedded as base64 — no server, no network). Each page compares the three sandhi modes (pre, g2p ★, post) against the off baseline. English pages (_en) are translations of the originals; the linguistic examples are identical.
